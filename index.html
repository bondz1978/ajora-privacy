🌿 AJora

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

📋 Overview

AJora ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by AJ-Production and is available on the Google Play Store. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the AJora application.

🔒 Most data in AJora is stored locally on your device and is never sent to our servers.

📦 Data We Collect

AJora collects and stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Display name and profile photo (optional, user-provided)
  • Date of birth (optional, for age-related features)
  • Last Quran reading position and bookmarks
  • App preferences (theme, language, selected reciter)

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this data on external servers.

🔑 App Permissions

AJora requests the following device permissions:

📷
Camera Used to take a profile photo. Not used for any other purpose.
🖼️
Storage / Gallery Used to select a profile photo from your gallery.
📍
Location Used for map features and prayer time calculation based on your location.
🌐
Internet Required to fetch Quran data, news feeds, AI responses, and display ads.
🔔
Notifications Used for prayer time reminders (if enabled by user).

👶 Children's Privacy

AJora is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can remove it.

🛡️ Data Security

All user preferences and personal data in AJora are stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. We do not operate any backend servers that store your personal data. Data transmitted to third-party services (e.g. Groq for AI) is subject to their respective privacy policies.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
